Teaching and outreach

austin ~/austin-ellis $ cat outreach/README

Outside my own research

I help other students get into science. That means helping run a journal club, talking with undergrads about how to join a lab, and showing visiting high school and middle school students what research looks like.

clubs/

Journal Club and Chemistry Club

journal-club after a materials chemistry discussion

I co-founded CSUN’s first Chemistry & Biochemistry Journal Club and am its vice president. I help pick the papers, find speakers, and sort out logistics. At meetings we go through a paper’s figures together, and students take turns presenting.

chemistry-club “Breaking Science” talk

I also help with Chemistry Club events. For one of their “Breaking Science” sessions, I gave a talk on the 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics.

events/

Campus events

undergrad-research Office of Undergraduate Research

Getting into research

At Office of Undergraduate Research events, including CSUN’s yearly Getting Into Research day, I talk with students about how to join a lab, find a mentor, and apply to research programs.

demos Chemistry Club and ACS

Chemistry demos

I help run hands-on demo tables at Chemistry Club and ACS events. It’s a relaxed way for students to see some chemistry up close and talk with other students about it.

campus-visits CSUNposium · CSUN Science Day

Campus visits

When high school students came for CSUNposium, I welcomed them as they toured the research posters, labs, and campus. At CSUN Science Day, I introduced local middle schoolers to high-pressure chemistry.
