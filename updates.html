November 2026

Preprint on pretrained interatomic potentials

On November 22, we posted Accuracy and Limits of Pretrained Interatomic Potentials Across Molecular Reaction Pathways on ChemRxiv. I am the first author, and the work was done during my internship at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. We compared fifteen pretrained interatomic potentials with DFT on 9,017 gas-phase reaction pathways. The models trained on molecular data are the most accurate, and the best of them, UMA-M OMol, reproduces 96% of 3,713 CHNO barriers within 1 kcal mol−1.

November 2026

Cs–Ba–O preprint

On November 4, our preprint Core Reactivity in Cs–Ba–O Ternary System under Pressure was posted on arXiv. Samantha Ellis is the first author and I am the second of five authors. High pressure can bring the Cs 5p core electrons into covalent bonding. Our structure searches at 150 and 250 GPa find CsBa3O8 and CsBaO4 on the convex hull at 250 GPa, with weak Cs 5p–O 2p bonding and intact O2 units. Cs2BaO3 shows stronger bonding but is metastable.

October 2026

WISP preprint

On October 27, I posted my sole-author preprint WISP: Learning Spin-Resolved Electron Localization from Atomic Environments on ChemRxiv. WISP is a network that predicts the electron localization function of both spin channels at any point in a crystal from the atomic structure and the magnetic moments on the atoms. On 21,922 held-out structures, the mean absolute error is 0.011 per channel on the ELF scale of 0 to 1.

September 2026

ELFNet paper published

On September 16, my first-author paper Prediction of Electron Localization Functions from Superposed Atomic Densities for Accelerated Superhydride Discovery was published in the Journal of Materials Informatics. ELFNet is a periodic 3D convolutional network that maps inexpensive superposed atomic densities to DFT electron localization functions, so bonding and interstitial localization can be screened before a full calculation.

This is the full paper on the ELF work I first shared at NeurIPS AI4Mat 2025 and presented at APS in March.

July 2026

Hydrogen electride paper published

On July 8, my first-author paper Emerging Electride Behavior and Metallization in Molecular Hydrogen under High Pressure was published in The Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters. As solid hydrogen metallizes under pressure, electrons localize in the interstitial regions between H2 molecules, an electride-like route that sets it apart from other molecular crystals.

CSUN news features

Our group’s work on pressure-driven redox reversal of iron and its role in element distribution deep in Earth was featured by CSUN Newsroom. The piece highlights how computational evidence for iron’s redox reversal points to new bonding pathways for p-block elements under extreme pressure and can reshape models of core formation and evolution.
